## What will you learn in this lesson?

In this lesson we will explore:
- **Deep Theory:** Classes, primary and secondary constructors with `init` blocks, visibility modifiers (`public`, `internal`, `protected`, `private`), `open` vs `final` classes (closed by default), interfaces, and composition over inheritance.
- **Under the Hood:** How Kotlin compiles classes as `final` by default in Bytecode and automatically generates getters/setters for properties.
- **Incremental Lab (AI Chat CLI):** Encapsulating the core engine (`ChatEngine`) and abstracting internal chat services behind cohesive interfaces.
