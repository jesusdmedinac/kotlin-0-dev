## What will you learn in this lesson?

In this lesson we will explore:
- **Deep Theory:** Elements of `CoroutineContext`, safe thread switching with `withContext`, specific roles of `Dispatchers.IO`, `Dispatchers.Default`, and `Dispatchers.Unconfined`, and exception handling with `CoroutineExceptionHandler`.
- **Under the Hood:** How Dispatchers back onto adaptive JVM thread pools (`ForkJoinPool` / worker threads).
- **Incremental Lab (AI Chat CLI):** Isolating blocking network calls to remote AI APIs onto `Dispatchers.IO` while processing token streaming on `Dispatchers.Default`.
