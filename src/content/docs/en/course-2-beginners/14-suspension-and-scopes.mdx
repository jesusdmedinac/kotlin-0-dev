## What will you learn in this lesson?

In this lesson we will explore:
- **Deep Theory:** `suspend` functions, `Job` lifecycle, coroutine builders (`launch`, `async`/`await`), `coroutineScope`, and cooperative cancellation checking `isActive` / `yield`.
- **Under the Hood:** Compiler transformation to State Machines (Continuation Passing Style - CPS) and the hidden `Continuation<T>` parameter.
- **Incremental Lab (AI Chat CLI):** Building a concurrent "AI is typing..." console animation that renders smoothly alongside incoming response streams.
