This course is designed for engineers seeking to apply rigorous software engineering principles using Kotlin as their primary medium.

We will deconstruct design patterns, JVM architecture, bytecode optimization, compiler plugins, and performance profiling—examining why and how Kotlin became one of the industry's most expressive and reliable high-level languages.
