### Course Focus
This course approaches Kotlin as a full-fledged multiplatform ecosystem. **Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP)** and **Compose Multiplatform (CMP)** form the backbone of this curriculum.

The goal is to empower mobile developers to master the language across platforms, building deep intuition for the underlying compilation targets (JVM, iOS/Native via LLVM, and WebAssembly) and architecture engines.
